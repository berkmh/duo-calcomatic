Duo Calcomatic · last updated November 18, 2026

Support · Privacy Policy

What the app collects

Nothing. Duo Calcomatic has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information.

What stays on your device

Your settings (theme, display, sounds and the rest) and your best game scores are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Sums, the memory and the paper tape are kept only while the app is open. Deleting the app deletes everything.

The battery reading

The solar cell can show your battery level. The app reads it from iOS while it is open, uses it only to draw the cells, and never stores or sends it.

Network use

None. Everything, including the key sounds, is inside the app, and it works entirely offline.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, of any age.
