How it works

  • Half fold the phone and stand it like a little laptop: the display is on the upright half, the keys on the flat half. Opened flat, the display and keys sit side by side.
  • Closed, the outside screen is a pocket calculator that carries on the same sum, a clock, or a hard cover. Choose in Settings.
  • On other iPhones the display sits across the top and the keys below.
  • Settings is the small sliders button beside the solar cell.

Simple or scientific

  • Simple works left to right as you press, like a four-function desk calculator: 2 + 3 × 4 is 20.
  • Scientific multiplies before it adds (2 + 3 × 4 is 14) and adds brackets, powers, sin, cos, tan, logs, π, 1/x, n! and EXP. INV turns a key into its opposite; DRG switches degrees and radians.
  • Memory: M+ and M− add to and take from the memory; MRC recalls it, and pressing MRC twice clears it.
  • Percent: 200 + 10 % gives 220.

Looks, sounds and feel

  • Themes set the case colour, the display (grey LCD, green glow, red LED, amber or blue backlight) and the shape of the keys.
  • Key sounds are recordings of real mechanical keyboard switches. They follow the phone's silent switch.
  • Haptics are felt through the hand holding the phone. With the phone lying on a desk they can't be felt, so the key sound does the job there.
  • The solar cell shows your battery: each of the four cells is a quarter of the charge. Cover it with a finger and the display fades.
  • Paper tape prints each sum as you go; tap it to tear it off. It can be switched off.

Secrets

There are two hidden games and a couple of tricks. Settings → Games tells you how to find them.
